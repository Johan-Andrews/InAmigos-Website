🏛️ Who We Are

InAmigos Foundation

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization licensed by the Central Government — committed to creating lasting social impact across India.

Based in Chhattisgarh, we operate through a strong network of dedicated professionals and volunteers spanning 28 states. Our operations are backed by 80G & 12A certifications, ensuring transparency, accountability, and tax-exempt benefits for donors. We are also CSR-1 registered, NITI Aayog registered, and hold the prestigious IAF ISO 9001:2015 certification.

💛 Why It Matters

The Change We Drive

01
📚

Closing the Education Gap

Millions of children in India grow up without access to quality education. Through BachpanShala, we run learning centres that provide school support, digital literacy, and life-skills training — because education is the most powerful tool to break the cycle of poverty.

  • ✔ Digital literacy for underprivileged children
  • ✔ After-school tutoring centres
  • ✔ Life skills and vocational awareness
02
👩

Empowering Women, Strengthening Families

Project Udaan works with self-help groups in rural India to promote financial independence, entrepreneurship, and awareness around health and hygiene. When women rise, entire communities rise with them.

  • ✔ Self-help group collaborations in rural areas
  • ✔ Skill development & micro-entrepreneurship
  • ✔ Menstrual hygiene and health awareness drives
03
🌿

Protecting Our Planet

Climate change is an urgent threat. Project Prakriti leads tree-plantation drives, promotes eco-friendly farming, and educates communities on sustainability — because a healthy planet is the foundation for all human progress.

  • ✔ 20,000+ saplings planted across India
  • ✔ Eco-agriculture awareness campaigns
  • ✔ World Environment Day & Water Day events
